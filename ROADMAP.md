## Philosophy

The Master Orchestrator is built on the belief that AI can be a true partner in building something extraordinary. The roadmap reflects:

1. **Autonomy** - Increasing the agent's ability to act independently
2. **Integration** - Connecting to real systems that matter
3. **Intelligence** - Getting smarter through learning and adaptation
4. **Reliability** - Being trustworthy at scale
5. **Humanity** - Keeping humans in control of decisions that matter

We're building not a replacement for human judgment, but a partner that amplifies human capability and enables execution at scale.
